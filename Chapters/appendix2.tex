\chapter{Informed Consent Form}
\label{app:consent}

\textbf{Purpose}: You are invited to participate in a research study exploring user experiences in virtual reality environments. This study aims to understand how people respond to and interact with VR technology.

\textbf{Procedure}: During this study, you will be asked to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Wear a VR headset for approximately 15-30 minutes;
    \item Complete a series of simple tasks within the virtual environment
    \item Fill out questionnaires about your experience before and after
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Risks}: Some participants may experience mild discomfort such as dizziness, nausea, or eye strain during or after VR use. You may take breaks or discontinue participation at any time if you feel uncomfortable.

\textbf{Benefits}: You will gain experience with VR technology and contribute to research advancing our understanding of virtual reality interactions.

\textbf{Confidentiality}: All data collected will be kept confidential. Results may be used for research purposes, but your identity will remain anonymous.

\textbf{Voluntary Participation}: Your participation is completely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without penalty.

\textbf{Contact}: If you have questions about this research, please contact the researcher conducting this study.
